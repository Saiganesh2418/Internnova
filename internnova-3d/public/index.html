Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding experience?

Basic understanding helps, but our programs are designed to take you from fundamentals to advanced concepts through practical projects.

Will I get a verified certificate?

Yes, every completion certificate comes with a unique Verification ID that employers can check directly on our platform.

Are the internships paid?

The core learning internships are unpaid skill-building programs. However, top performers may receive paid freelance opportunities or stipends based on their project performance.

How does the placement support work?

For Pro Internships, we help you build an ATS-friendly resume, optimize your portfolio, conduct mock interviews, and share your profile with our hiring partners.

How long does each internship program last?

Our virtual internship tracks run for 6 weeks, structured into 4 comprehensive milestones with flexible submission timelines to balance with college exams.

Can I do multiple domain internships simultaneously?

We recommend enrolling in one domain track at a time to ensure dedicated focus on high quality project submissions and capstone completion.
